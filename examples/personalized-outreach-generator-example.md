# LinkedIn Message — Example

**Name:** Maneesh Goyal
**Title:** Chief Operating Officer, Mayo Clinic Platform
**Company:** Mayo Clinic Platform
**Context:** Mayo Clinic Platform launched Platform_Insights in November 2025 — a global B2B SaaS product sold to health systems. Goyal owns financial performance and operational growth of the platform. CIO seat has been vacant since November 2024.
**Angle:** Running an enterprise SaaS sales motion inside a clinical institution — the CRM infrastructure likely wasn't built for it.

---

### Version A — Warm / Relationship-First

Maneesh — launching Platform_Insights into a global market is a real inflection point, not just a product milestone. Most platform businesses at this stage are running their sales motion on systems that were designed for something else entirely — and it stays invisible until pipeline starts moving faster than the infrastructure supporting it. I work with a small number of orgs on exactly this: making sure the CRM and sales ops layer reflects the commercial motion, not the institutional history. Nothing prescriptive — just noticing the timing and thought it was worth a mention. Happy to share what I'm seeing from similar transitions if useful.

---

### Version B — Direct / Slightly Provocative

Maneesh — selling AI tools to health systems in four countries is a fundamentally different motion than anything Mayo's CRM was built to support. The stage logic, deal structure, and account visibility required for a global SaaS sale don't map cleanly onto systems designed around patient relationships or institutional procurement. Most orgs in this position discover the gap mid-quarter, when forecast conversations stop adding up. We specialize in fixing that exact problem — the Salesforce layer that didn't get rebuilt when the business model changed. Worth a conversation before it becomes a fire drill?

---

## Notes

- Version A is better for a cold first touch — it observes without asserting
- Version B works if there's a warm intro or a clear trigger event that makes the pain more obvious
- "Happy to share what I'm seeing" (Version A) is lower friction than "worth a conversation" (Version B)
- Neither version mentions Foundree42 by name — in a LinkedIn DM, the profile does that work
- If Goyal has posted about platform growth or operational scaling recently, open with a specific reference to that instead of the product launch
