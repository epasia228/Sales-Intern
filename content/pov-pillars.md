# Foundree42 — POV Pillars

The beliefs that anchor all content, outreach, and positioning. Every post, brief, and message should connect back to at least one of these.

---

## 1. Enforcement > Advice

AI that advises doesn't change behavior. AI that enforces does.
The moment you move from "here's what you should do" to "the system won't let you skip this," you expose every gap in process, data, and ownership. Most teams avoid that moment. We design for it.

**Shows up as:**
- Pipeline discipline enforced through system logic, not coaching
- Stage progression tied to required fields, not suggestions
- Agentforce designed to act, not to surface insights

---

## 2. Clarity Before Code

You can't automate a broken process. You can only make it fail faster.
Before any AI or Salesforce build, there has to be a clear answer to: what does good look like, who owns the outcome, and what does the system actually need to do?

**Shows up as:**
- Discovery that exposes process debt before scoping work
- No build without defined ownership and outcome definition
- Rejecting projects where the client wants output before clarity

---

## 3. Crawl/Walk/Run Is a Delay Strategy

Phased rollouts feel responsible. In practice, they produce pilots, demos, and side panels — while the team keeps working the same way they always have.
The alternative isn't recklessness. It's treating implementation like training: start small, pick something that matters, enforce early, refine fast.

**Shows up as:**
- Pipeline discipline as the first AI use case, not the last
- First use case designed to perform, not to be safe
- Speed through early enforcement, not delayed visibility

---

## 4. Data Ownership Is a People Problem

Dirty data isn't a technical issue. It's an accountability issue.
If no one owns the outcome, the agent won't either. Data quality follows ownership clarity — not the other way around.

**Shows up as:**
- Ownership definition as a prerequisite to any CRM or AI engagement
- Validation and field logic that enforces accountability
- Rejecting "get our data right first" as a precondition to starting

---

## 5. Outcomes Over Output

Demos, dashboards, and side panels are output.
Forecast accuracy, pipeline discipline, and rep behavior change are outcomes.
The difference between a Salesforce org that works and one that doesn't is usually not features — it's whether the system is designed around outcomes anyone actually cares about.

**Shows up as:**
- Scoping conversations that start with "what does success look like in 90 days"
- Avoiding feature-led implementations
- Measuring engagement by behavior change, not tool adoption

---

## Usage

- Every LinkedIn post should have a clear POV pillar it anchors to
- Every outreach message should reflect at least one pillar implicitly
- Every account brief hypothesis should connect to a pillar
- When content feels generic, map it back to these and sharpen
