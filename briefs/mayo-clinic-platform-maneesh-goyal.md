# Account Brief — Mayo Clinic Platform
**Contact:** Maneesh Goyal — Chief Operating Officer, Mayo Clinic Platform
**Signal:** Launch of Platform_Insights (November 2025) — global B2B SaaS product sold to health systems worldwide
**Brief Date:** March 2026

---

### 1. Company Snapshot

Mayo Clinic Platform is the commercial arm of Mayo Clinic, operating as a de facto B2B SaaS business inside a $21.5B clinical institution. It licenses AI clinical tools, data frameworks, and research infrastructure to health systems globally across three products: Platform_Insights, Platform_Orchestrate, and Platform_Accelerate. The platform has real enterprise customers, global reach (Brazil, Israel, Canada, South Korea), and $1B+ in AI investment behind it — but it is, institutionally, a startup running inside one of the world's most conservative organizations.

---

### 2. Contact Context

Goyal owns financial performance, operational growth, and AI investment decisions for Mayo Clinic Platform — the function closest to "GM of a software business" within a nonprofit health system. He is measured on platform revenue, partner activation, and the speed at which AI products reach paying health system customers. His pressure is structural: he is running a commercial software motion inside an organization whose DNA, systems, and culture were built entirely around clinical care delivery.

---

### 3. Signal Interpretation

Platform_Insights launching in November 2025 means Mayo now has a product in market being actively sold to health systems worldwide — which requires pipeline management, account tracking, contract visibility, and forecast reporting at a level a clinical CRM or spreadsheet-based approach cannot support. The team running this (Harnisch on provider sales, Bethke on solution developer/vendor sales) is staffed like a commercial operation, but the underlying CRM infrastructure almost certainly reflects the institution that built it, not the sales motion it needs to support. A 12-year CIO just retired, the CDO is 4 months into the role, and the CIO seat is still vacant — nobody owns enterprise system coherence right now.

---

### 4. Core Hypothesis

Mayo Clinic Platform is executing a global enterprise SaaS sales motion on CRM infrastructure that was designed around patient relationship management and institutional processes — not pipeline stages, deal velocity, or multi-stakeholder enterprise sales cycles — and the gap is widening as they add international partners and new product lines.

---

### 5. Angle

Health systems that launch commercial platform businesses typically underestimate how different the CRM requirements are from their clinical or operational systems — the deal structures, buying committees, and pipeline logic for selling AI tools to a health system in Brazil have nothing in common with managing patient relationships in Rochester. That mismatch usually stays invisible until forecast calls stop making sense or a partner relationship falls through a gap in handoffs. Curious whether the sales infrastructure has kept pace with where Platform_Insights is now.
