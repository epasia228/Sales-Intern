# Account Brief — Virta Health
**Contact:** Nathan Gage — VP of Commercial Operations
**Signal:** Hiring first-ever Salesforce Administrator + VP Sales posting requiring CRM implementation experience + 80%+ YoY revenue growth
**Brief Date:** April 2026

---

### 1. Company Snapshot

Virta Health is a B2B virtual care platform reversing type 2 diabetes and treating obesity — $158M ARR, 80%+ YoY growth, 550+ employer and health plan clients covering 12M+ US lives. They sell primarily to self-insured employers and health plans, with recent expansion into government payers (CMS). 833 employees, Series E funded (~$350M raised). They are in an aggressive commercial growth phase with a widening product portfolio (diabetes reversal + weight loss) and a sales infrastructure that is visibly lagging behind the revenue trajectory.

---

### 2. Contact Context

Gage owns commercial operations — pipeline management, forecasting, CRM systems, and the infrastructure that supports a B2B sales team selling to enterprise employers and health plans. He is measured on commercial velocity, data quality, and the organization's ability to forecast and report accurately at a growth rate that doubles revenue year-over-year. His immediate pressure: Virta is hiring its first dedicated Salesforce Administrator, which means Gage (or someone above him) has recognized the org is too lean for the motion they're running — and is now trying to build the foundation while the business keeps scaling.

---

### 3. Signal Interpretation

"Hiring first Salesforce Administrator" is not a routine ops hire — it's an acknowledgment that the CRM has outgrown whoever was managing it informally. At 80% YoY growth with Salesforce, HubSpot, SalesLoft, and LeanData all integrated, the data flowing between those systems is almost certainly inconsistent. Multiple tools with overlapping functions and no dedicated admin means pipeline data is unreliable, attribution is guesswork, and the sales team is compensating manually. The VP Sales posting explicitly calls out CRM implementation experience — meaning leadership knows the foundation needs to be rebuilt, not just maintained.

---

### 4. Core Hypothesis

Virta built their commercial stack fast to keep pace with growth — Salesforce plus three integrated tools with no dedicated admin — and is now at the stage where the seams are showing in forecast accuracy and pipeline visibility, right as they're trying to scale the sales team and expand into government payers.

---

### 5. Angle

Most high-growth digital health companies hit a specific moment: the commercial stack that worked at $50M ARR — Salesforce connected to HubSpot, SalesLoft, and LeanData, managed by whoever had the most Salesforce access — stops being reliable at $150M when the sales team doubles and enterprise deal cycles get longer. The symptoms are usually the same: pipeline data nobody fully trusts, attribution that doesn't add up, and a forecast that requires manual reconciliation before every board call. Hiring a first Salesforce Admin is the right call, but it doesn't fix the underlying process and data model issues — it just gives someone ownership of a system that still needs to be rebuilt around how the business actually sells. Curious whether you're seeing that tension as you scale the commercial team.
