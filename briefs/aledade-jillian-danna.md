# Account Brief — Aledade Inc
**Contact:** Jillian D'Anna — Senior Director, Sales Operations
**Signal:** CCA Holding Company acquisition (June 2025) + $500M credit facility closed + new Chief Commercial Officer hired
**Brief Date:** April 2026

---

### 1. Company Snapshot

Aledade is the largest independent primary care network in the US — 20,000+ clinicians, 2,400+ practices across 46 states, nearly 3M Medicare patients under value-based care. They are well-funded ($1.18B raised, $500M credit facility), acquisition-active (CCA Michigan operations June 2025, Medical Advantage prior year), and in the middle of a deliberate commercial buildout: new CCO hired, new CTO hired, $500M in dry powder. This is a company accelerating its commercial infrastructure, not just its clinical network.

---

### 2. Contact Context

D'Anna owns the operational engine behind Aledade's sales motion — pipeline process, CRM hygiene, forecasting, and the systems that connect commercial activity to revenue. She is measured on sales velocity, data quality, and the reliability of the commercial reporting that leadership uses to make growth decisions. Her pressure is structural: Aledade is adding practices, geographies, and payer relationships faster than most commercial ops teams can keep pace with, and the CCA acquisition just added another layer of complexity to an already distributed operation.

---

### 3. Signal Interpretation

A new CCO hire, a $500M credit facility, and a mid-year acquisition in the same 12-month window means Aledade's commercial motion is being rebuilt at scale — not maintained. That kind of acceleration almost always exposes gaps in CRM data quality, pipeline stage logic, and forecasting reliability that were manageable at the prior scale but aren't anymore. The CCA acquisition in particular brings new practices, new payer relationships, and new commercial activity that needs to be onboarded into whatever system Aledade's sales ops team is running. If the CCO is new and the credit facility is fresh, the mandate to invest in commercial infrastructure is live right now.

---

### 4. Core Hypothesis

Aledade's sales ops layer was built for a company growing through organic network expansion — it wasn't designed for the multi-acquisition, multi-payer, multi-state commercial motion they're running now, and a new CCO with capital to deploy will surface that gap quickly.

---

### 5. Angle

Value-based care networks that grow through acquisition tend to hit the same wall: the commercial operations infrastructure reflects the original model — direct practice outreach, ACO enrollment, relationship-managed payer deals — not the scaled, multi-channel motion that emerges after two or three acquisitions and a new C-suite. The first thing a new CCO usually asks for is a clean forecast and a reliable pipeline view. Whether Aledade's current setup can deliver that cleanly is worth knowing before the question gets asked in a board meeting. Curious whether the CCA integration surfaced anything on that front.
