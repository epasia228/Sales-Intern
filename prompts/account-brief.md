# Account Brief Prompt (Foundree42)

You are generating a sharp, executive-ready account brief for outreach and conversation preparation.

The goal is NOT to pitch.
The goal is to surface a clear, differentiated point of view that creates a reason to engage.

Write with clarity, specificity, and restraint.
Avoid generic consulting language.

---

## Inputs

Company:
Contact (Name + Title):
Signal to Anchor On:
(Examples: hiring surge, AI initiative, Salesforce expansion, leadership change, M&A, etc.)

Notes (raw paste):
(LinkedIn bio, company site, news, prior context, etc.)

---

## Output Format (5 Sections Only)

### 1. Company Snapshot
2–3 sentences max.

- What the company does
- Where they likely are in maturity / scale
- Keep it factual, grounded, and concise

---

### 2. Contact Context
2–3 sentences max.

- What this person likely owns
- What they are measured on
- What pressures they are likely under

---

### 3. Signal Interpretation
2–4 sentences max.

- What the signal actually suggests (not just what it is)
- Why it matters operationally
- What is likely happening beneath the surface

Avoid restating the signal. Interpret it.

---

### 4. Core Hypothesis (One Only)
1–2 sentences.

- What is most likely *not working as well as it should*
- Tie directly to execution, not strategy
- Be specific, not broad

This should feel like an insight, not a guess.

---

### 5. Angle (Observation → Implication → Open)
3–5 sentences total.

Structure:
- Observation: A grounded, specific pattern seen in similar situations
- Implication: What that typically leads to (risk, inefficiency, missed outcome)
- Open: A neutral, non-pitch question to invite conversation

Rules:
- Do NOT pitch services
- Do NOT suggest a meeting
- Do NOT sound salesy
- This should feel like a thoughtful observation, not outreach

---

## Writing Rules

- Be concise. No fluff.
- No buzzwords (e.g., "leverage", "synergy", "transformational")
- No generic statements that could apply to any company
- Prioritize specificity over completeness
- Sound like an operator, not a consultant

---

## Tone

- Calm, confident, and direct
- Insightful without being provocative for the sake of it
- Written for an executive audience
