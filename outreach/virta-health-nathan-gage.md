# LinkedIn Outreach — Virta Health
**Contact:** Nathan Gage — VP of Commercial Operations
**Signal:** Hiring first-ever Salesforce Administrator + VP Sales posting requiring CRM implementation experience + 80%+ YoY revenue growth
**Angle:** Commercial stack built fast for early employer sales — now running a scaled, multi-product motion on a data model that wasn't designed for it

---

### Version A — Warm / Relationship-First

Nathan — hiring a first Salesforce Admin is the right call at Virta's growth stage, but it's also a signal that the commercial infrastructure is at an inflection point. At 80% YoY growth with Salesforce, HubSpot, SalesLoft, and LeanData all in the stack, the question is usually less about administration and more about whether the underlying data model and process logic can support where the sales team is going next. We've worked with digital health companies at exactly this moment — not rebuilding from scratch, but making sure the foundation the new admin inherits is one that actually works. Happy to share what tends to surface at this stage if it's useful.

---

### Version B — Direct / Slightly Provocative

Nathan — most digital health companies at $150M ARR are running a commercial stack that was built for $50M: Salesforce connected to HubSpot, SalesLoft, and LeanData, managed by whoever had admin access, and held together by manual reconciliation before every forecast call. Hiring a first admin is the right move, but it doesn't fix the data model — it just gives someone ownership of a system that still needs to be rebuilt around how the business actually sells today. With a VP Sales search underway and a CMS expansion on the horizon, that gap tends to widen fast. Curious whether the admin role is the whole answer or just the first step.

---

## Usage Notes

- Version A is the right cold opener — acknowledges the hire as a positive signal without being critical
- Version B is sharper and works better if he's posted about commercial scaling, the admin search, or the GLP-1 market opportunity
- **The "first admin" framing is intentional** — it signals you did your homework without being aggressive about it
- If he doesn't respond, the COO (Amit Shah) is the secondary path — frame around operational scaling, not CRM specifically
- Don't mention the specific integrations (HubSpot, SalesLoft, LeanData) in cold outreach — save that for the conversation; it signals too much research upfront
